Spring-Pole Mechanics & Critical Hazard Neutralization

Trees uprooted or severed during high-wind storms in Roswell, Georgia often land under tremendous bending compression. When a multi-ton oak trunk is pinned beneath other fallen limbs or bent over residential roof structures, it forms an active "spring-pole"—a mechanical catapult containing thousands of foot-pounds of stored kinetic energy.

Cutting into compressed or tensioned timber without specialized boring cuts or top kerf relief causes chainsaws to pinch and trunks to explode violently outward. Our ISA-certified tree crews execute computerized load-vector assessments and staged bypass cuts to systematically neutralize mechanical tension before severance.

Engineered Rigging & Zero-Impact Extraction

  • Staged Kerf De-Tensioning: Precision release cuts relieve hydraulic and mechanical stress in pinned timber.
  • Hydraulic Crane Off-Loading: Heavy-tonnage cranes support the suspended load prior to the final severance cut.
  • Post-Clearance Weatherproofing: Immediate heavy industrial tarpaulin deployment over roof punctures stops water intrusion.
